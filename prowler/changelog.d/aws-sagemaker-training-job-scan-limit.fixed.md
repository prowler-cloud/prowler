AWS SageMaker service init no longer runs a per-job `DescribeTrainingJob` and `ListTags` call for every training job an account has ever run; a new `aws.max_sagemaker_training_jobs` config option (falling back to `aws.max_scanned_resources_per_service`) caps how many of the newest jobs are hydrated, so accounts with hundreds of thousands of undeletable training jobs no longer hang or OOM a scan
