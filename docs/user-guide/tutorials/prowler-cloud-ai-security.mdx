The AI Security page brings together every AI check that Prowler runs, whatever provider it belongs to. It answers the question **"How securely are the AI vendors and AI services of the organization configured, and what should be fixed first?"**

<SubscriptionBanner />

The page covers two layers of the AI stack:

* **Model vendors:** Claude, OpenAI and ChatGPT organizations, added as providers from the Registry. Every check of these providers counts as an AI check.
* **Cloud AI services:** Amazon Bedrock and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore in AWS, Azure AI services and Azure OpenAI in Azure, and Vertex AI in Google Cloud. Only the checks in the `gen-ai` category of these providers count.

<Note>
In Prowler Private Cloud, the model vendor providers and the AI compliance frameworks are installed from the Registry, so they require the Registry to be enabled in the installation.
</Note>

## Opening AI Security

To open the page, sign in to Prowler Cloud at [cloud.prowler.com](https://cloud.prowler.com/sign-in) and select **AI Security** in the left navigation.

The filters at the top work as on the Overview page: to narrow every section of the page, select providers, accounts or a provider group. Every link to the Findings page keeps these filters and shows the failing, unmuted findings in the `gen-ai` category.

In Prowler Local Server, **AI Security** appears in the left navigation as a Prowler Cloud feature and opens the upgrade dialog.

## Key Figures

The three cards at the top summarize the latest scans of the providers in scope:

* **Pass findings:** The percentage of passing findings among the passing and failing ones, with the totals of each.
* **Failed findings:** The number of failing findings, split by severity. To open the findings of a severity, click it.
* **New failed findings:** The failing findings that were not in the previous scan. To open them, click "View new findings".

## Your AI Stack

This section lists each provider type of both layers, with the number of failing findings per layer. A row shows one of the following states:

* **Not connected:** No provider of that type has been added. To add one, click "Add Provider". Adding a provider requires the "Manage Providers" permission.
* **No completed scan yet:** The provider has been added but has no completed scan. To start one, click "Launch scan".
* **No AI services found:** The provider has been scanned, but the scan returned no AI findings, for example because the cloud account runs none of the AI services that Prowler checks.
* **Results:** A bar with the share of passing findings, the passing and total counts, and the number of findings that need manual review. To open the failing findings of that provider type, click the percentage.

To filter the whole page to one provider type, click its row. To show every AI provider again, click the same row.

<Note>
Each layer is scanned through its own provider. A gap in one layer stays open, however well the other layer scores.
</Note>

## Compliance Frameworks

This section shows the AI compliance frameworks installed in the tenant, with the same cards as the Compliance page. These frameworks are distributed through the Registry in the **AI Security** bundle:

* OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025
* OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026
* OWASP MCP Top 10 2025
* NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) 1.0
* ISO/IEC 42001:2023
* EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

Each card aggregates the framework across every compatible provider type, using the latest completed scan of each provider. To open the detail of a framework, click its card. To go to the Compliance page, click "Open in Compliance". See [Cross-Provider Compliance](/user-guide/compliance/tutorials/cross-provider-compliance).

When none of these frameworks is installed, the section shows "No AI frameworks installed". To install them, click "Install from Registry" or "Browse Registry". Both open the compliance packages in the Registry.

## Top Risks

This section lists up to six failed AI checks, ranked as on the Findings page: by severity first, then by new and changed failures. Each row shows the severity, the check title and identifier, the affected provider types and the number of failing resources.

To open the failing findings of a check, click its title. To open every failing AI finding in scope, click "View all AI findings".

## When the Page Shows No Results

When no Claude, OpenAI, ChatGPT, AWS, Azure or Google Cloud provider is in scope, the page shows "No AI findings yet". To fill the page, add one of these providers and scan it, or widen the filters.
