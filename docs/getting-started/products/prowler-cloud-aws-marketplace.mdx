This page explains how to subscribe to **Prowler Cloud** through the **AWS Marketplace**, how to activate the subscription in Prowler Cloud, how billing works, and how to fix the errors you may hit on the way.

## Plans

Prowler Cloud has two listings in the AWS Marketplace. Both give you the same product. They differ in how you commit and how you pay.

A **provider** is a cloud account you connect to Prowler Cloud: an AWS account, an Azure subscription, a GCP project, a Kubernetes cluster, and so on.

| | Monthly (pay as you go) | Annual (billed annually) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing | [Prowler Cloud $99/provider per month (Pay as You Go)](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-nmbvelz746oyk) | [Prowler Cloud $79/provider per month (Billed Annually)](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-wwehsmzui25ri) |
| Billing | Monthly, per connected provider | Once, at checkout, for the full 12 months |
| Commitment | None. Cancel anytime. | 12 months for the number of providers you choose during checkout on AWS. |
| Resources beyond the included amount | Billed per resource | Billed per resource, at the annual rate |
| Providers beyond your commitment | Not applicable | Billed monthly at the pay-as-you-go rate |

For prices, included resources and per-resource rates, see the [Prowler Cloud pricing page](https://prowler.com/pricing/).

## Before you subscribe

- **One AWS account pays for the whole organization.** Subscribe from a single AWS account. You then connect as many providers as you need, from any cloud, inside Prowler Cloud. You do not subscribe again for each AWS account you scan.
- **Decide which Prowler Cloud organization will pay.** Activation links the AWS purchase to the organization you are signed in to. If you belong to several organizations, switch to the right one before you activate. See [Activate the subscription in Prowler Cloud](#activate-the-subscription-in-prowler-cloud).
- **Annual plan: decide the provider count first.** AWS asks for it during checkout, and AWS charges the full year at that moment. Providers you add beyond that count are billed monthly at the pay-as-you-go rate. AWS does not let you cancel an annual contract yourself from the console. See [Cancel the subscription](#cancel-the-subscription) before you commit.
- **Cancel any card-billed subscription first.** An organization already billed by credit card cannot switch to AWS Marketplace billing until that subscription is cancelled. This prevents double charges.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the purchase and subscribe">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Monthly">
        Scroll to **Purchase details** and click **Subscribe**. There is no upfront charge. AWS bills your usage at the end of each month.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/purchase-monthly.png" alt="Purchase details for the monthly listing with the Subscribe button highlighted" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Annual">
        Under **Pricing details**, select the **providers** dimension and enter the **Number of units**. One unit is one provider for the 12-month term. Review the **Contract total**, then click **Subscribe** at the bottom of the page. AWS charges the full year now.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/purchase-annual.png" alt="Purchase options for the annual listing showing the contract details, the providers dimension and the Number of units table" />
        </Frame>

        **Auto-renewal** is on by default. Leave it on to have AWS renew the contract for another 12 months at the end date, or choose **Do not automatically renew this contract**. You can change this from **Manage subscriptions** any time before the renewal date.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Set up your account">
    AWS confirms the purchase and shows a **Set up your account** button. Click it. AWS sends your browser to Prowler Cloud with a one-time setup link.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/set-up-your-account.png" alt="AWS Marketplace purchase confirmation banner with the Set up your account button" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
    The setup link is single use and expires quickly: AWS documents roughly one hour, and never more than four. If you close the tab, press back, or need to start over, return to [Manage subscriptions](https://console.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/subscriptions) in the AWS Marketplace console, open **Prowler Cloud**, and click **Set up your account** again to get a fresh link.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate the subscription in Prowler Cloud

Nothing is linked until you confirm it in Prowler Cloud. The purchase is safe on the AWS side while you complete these steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in or create your account">
    Prowler Cloud shows the sign-in page with a banner that reads "To finish setting up your AWS Marketplace subscription, sign in or register if you don't have an account yet."

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/sign-in-marketplace.png" alt="Prowler Cloud sign-in page with the AWS Marketplace banner" />
    </Frame>

    If you do not have an account yet, click **Sign up**. The purchase carries over once you sign in with the new account.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/sign-up-marketplace.png" alt="Prowler Cloud sign-up page with the AWS Marketplace banner" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the organization and activate">
    The billing page shows a card titled **Activate your AWS Marketplace subscription**. It names the organization the purchase will be linked to.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/activate-confirm.png" alt="Activation card naming the organization, with the Activate subscription button and the Continue to billing without activating link" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
    Read the organization name before you click. Activation is a billing decision: charges for that organization go to the AWS account you subscribed with. If it is not the organization you want to pay for, switch organizations first, then come back to the billing page.
    </Warning>

    Click **Activate subscription**. Prowler Cloud links the purchase and activates the subscription. Two short status messages appear while it works: "Linking your AWS Marketplace purchase…" and "Activating your subscription…".

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/activate-working.png" alt="Activation card showing the Activating your subscription progress message" />
    </Frame>

    **Continue to billing without activating** discards the setup link and leaves the organization unchanged. Use it only if you landed in the wrong organization. To activate later, get a fresh link from the AWS Marketplace console as described above.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the subscription is active">
    The billing page now states that you subscribed through AWS Marketplace, shows the AWS account that pays, and links to your subscription in the AWS Marketplace console.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/billing-subscribed.png" alt="Prowler Cloud billing page for an AWS Marketplace subscription, showing the paying AWS account and the link to the AWS Marketplace console" />
    </Frame>

    There is no payment method, invoice history, or cancel button on this page. AWS handles all of that.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If something goes wrong during activation, see [Activation errors](#activation-errors).

## After activation

- Add providers from the **Providers** page in Prowler Cloud. Every provider you add is covered by the same AWS subscription, whatever cloud it belongs to.
- All charges come from AWS. Find them under **Amazon Web Services, Inc. - Marketplace** in [AWS Billing](https://console.aws.amazon.com/billing/home#/bills).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/marketplace-billing.png" alt="AWS Billing page showing the Amazon Web Services, Inc. - Marketplace line" />

### Monthly

AWS bills you at the end of each month for the providers connected and the resources scanned in Prowler Cloud during that month.

### Annual

AWS charges the full 12-month subscription once, at checkout. The providers covered by your commitment cost nothing more during the year. Your monthly AWS bill only includes usage beyond the commitment: extra providers at the pay-as-you-go rate, and resources beyond the included amount at the annual plan's per-resource rate.

If auto-renewal is on, AWS renews the contract for another 12 months at the end date and charges the new term the same way. Turn it off from **Manage subscriptions** before the renewal date if you do not want that.

## Cancel the subscription

There is no cancel button in Prowler Cloud for AWS Marketplace subscriptions. How you cancel depends on the plan, following the [AWS Marketplace cancellation rules](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/buyerguide/cancel-subscription.html).

### Monthly

Cancel from the AWS Marketplace console at any time:

1. Open [Manage subscriptions](https://console.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/subscriptions) and open **Prowler Cloud**.
2. Under **Active agreements**, select the agreement and click **View details**.
3. Open the **Actions** menu and choose **Cancel subscription**.
4. Type `confirm` and choose **Yes, cancel subscription**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/aws-manage-subscriptions.png" alt="Prowler Cloud agreement page in the AWS Marketplace console with the Actions menu open on Cancel subscription" />
</Frame>

Cancelling stops future charges. Usage accrued before the cancellation still appears on your next AWS bill.

### Annual

AWS does not offer self-service cancellation for annual contracts bought through a public listing.

- **Within 48 hours of purchase**, contact AWS Customer Service. AWS can cancel the contract and refund the full amount, but only if you ask for the refund.
- **After 48 hours**, contact [support@prowler.com](mailto:support@prowler.com). Prowler starts the cancellation request through AWS Marketplace and you approve it in the AWS Marketplace console. A refund is not automatic. Ask for it when you request the cancellation.

See [Refunds and cancellations in AWS Marketplace](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/buyerguide/buyer-refunds.html) for the AWS side of the process.

### What happens after cancellation

Once AWS ends the subscription, Prowler Cloud pauses scans for the organization. Providers, findings and scan history stay readable. A **Subscription required** card in the sidebar, labeled **Subscription cancelled**, explains it: "Scans are paused and your assessment data remains available. Subscribe again to continue scanning." The billing page shows the plan selection page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/aws-marketplace/billing-cancelled.png" alt="Prowler Cloud after an AWS Marketplace cancellation, showing the Subscription cancelled card in the sidebar and the plan selection page" />
</Frame>

To subscribe again from the same AWS account into the same Prowler Cloud organization, subscribe from the listing and activate as before.

<Warning>
Deleting your Prowler Cloud organization does not cancel the AWS subscription. AWS keeps charging the agreement until you cancel it as described above.
</Warning>

### SEPA payment method issues

If AWS Marketplace notifies that payment failed due to an issue with the payment method, this typically occurs when a SEPA bank account is set as the default payment method. AWS Marketplace does not support SEPA bank accounts for product subscriptions, even when the account includes valid alternative payment methods. This is because AWS Marketplace invoices are issued by AWS Inc., a US entity. SEPA accounts do not recognize these invoices as valid, causing subscription failures.

To successfully subscribe to AWS Marketplace products with a SEPA account configuration:

1. Switch default payment method to credit card
2. Complete subscription
3. Switch the default payment method back to the bank account

<Warning>
**Renewal Considerations**

This issue will recur during subscription renewals. AWS service teams recommend maintaining credit card as the default payment method to prevent future disruptions. Update payment methods at https://console.aws.amazon.com/billing/home#/paymentmethods.

</Warning>

<Note>
**AWS Marketplace Statement**

The AWS Marketplace team acknowledges this limitation: "We apologize for these additional steps, and please know we are fully aware of this situation, and our internal teams are working on simplifying this process."

</Note>

### Credit and debit card storage restrictions (AISPL customers in India)

AWS Marketplace no longer supports payments using credit or debit cards stored on file for Amazon Internet Services Private Limited (AISPL) customers. This restriction stems from Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations regarding payment aggregators, which prohibit the storage of card data. As explained in [this AWS blog post](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/awsmarketplace/restriction-on-credit-and-debit-card-purchases-for-aispl-customers-using-aws-marketplace/):

> AWS Marketplace can no longer support payments using credit or debit cards stored on file. [The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued a notice regarding regulation of payment aggregators](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11822&Mode=0), which restricts the storage of card data. If you are currently using credit or debit card as your default payment instrument, your ability to use AWS Marketplace products will be restricted. However, you can switch your default payment instrument to Pay By Invoice to avoid disruption or restore your original experience.

To maintain uninterrupted access to AWS Marketplace products, change the default payment instrument from stored card data to Pay By Invoice billing.
